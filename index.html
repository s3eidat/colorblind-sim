About colour blindness

Design tips

  • Never use colour alone to carry meaning: add labels, icons, patterns or position.
  • Avoid red against green (and green against brown). Blue with orange works for almost everyone.
  • Vary lightness, not just hue: if two colours look the same in Achromatopsia, they may clash for others too.
  • Charts: label lines directly, use dashes or markers, and keep legends close to the data.
  • Links and errors: underline links, and pair red error states with an icon and text.
